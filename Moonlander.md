## Design Philosophy

- Home row focused.
- Minimize hand movement.
- Heavy use of layers instead of reaching.
- Hyprland workspaces are controlled directly from the keyboard.

---

## Things Future Gary Will Forget

- F13–F18 are used for Hyprland workspaces.
- Shift + F13–F18 moves windows between workspaces.
